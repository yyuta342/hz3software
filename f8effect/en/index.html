What is F8 Effect?

Transform your live camera into a pixel art-like world in real time. Fine-tune visuals with ~100 color palettes and CRT effects, then capture your retro game world.

F8 Effect is a camera app that applies retro game-style effects to your live camera feed in real time. Experience the look and feel of 8-bit and 16-bit era visuals, right through your camera. You can also load images from your photo library and edit them with the same effects.

Drag the slider to see the difference

Key Features

  • ~100 Color Palettes
    Presets for instant retro looks, with full manual control over each setting
  • Resolution Presets
    Ranging from retro game styles to modern sizes
  • Contrast, Brightness & Color Inversion
    Fine-tune the look of your shot
  • Dithering
    Add that authentic pixel art texture
  • CRT-style Effect (RGB Mask)
    Recreate the look of an old CRT monitor
  • Vignette, Chromatic Aberration & Curvature
    Add a touch of vintage screen distortion
  • Edge Detection
    Create unique outlined looks

Why F8 Effect?

No fiddly settings required. Just pick a preset and let the color palettes and dithering do the work of creating that pixel art texture. Try a few out and find the look you like.

Great for retro gaming fans, and anyone curious about pixel art.
