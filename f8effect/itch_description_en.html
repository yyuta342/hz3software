Transform your live camera into a pixel art world in real time!

F8 Effect is a camera app that applies retro game-style effects to your live camera feed in real time. Experience the look and feel of 8-bit and 16-bit era visuals, right through your camera. You can also load images from your photo library and edit them with the same effects.

No fiddly settings required. Just pick a preset and let the color palettes and dithering do the work. Try a few out and find the look you like.

Key Features

  • ~100 Color Palettes — Presets for instant retro looks, with full manual control over each setting
  • 14 Retro Resolution Presets — PICO-8, Game Boy, NES, and more
  • Dithering — Add that authentic pixel art texture with various patterns
  • CRT-style Effect (RGB Mask) — Recreate the look of an old CRT monitor
  • Contrast, Brightness & Color Inversion — Fine-tune the look of your shot
  • Vignette, Chromatic Aberration & Curvature — Add a touch of vintage screen distortion
  • Edge Detection — Create unique outlined looks
  • Timer Capture — 3, 5, and 10 second timer supported
  • Photo Library Import — Apply effects to existing photos too
